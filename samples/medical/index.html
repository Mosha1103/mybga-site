Practice policies

Good to know

Cancellations and missed appointments

Please give at least 2 hours' notice for GP visits and 48 hours for allied health, so someone else can take the time. A fee may apply for missed appointments.

Your privacy

Your health information is kept private and secure, and only shared with your consent or where the law requires it. Ask reception for our full privacy policy.

Email and messages

Email is not checked around the clock, so never use it for urgent problems or appointments. Call the clinic, or 000 in an emergency.

Running late

Call us if you are running late. If you are more than 10 minutes late we may need to move your appointment.

Questions

Before you visit

Can I get a same-day appointment?

Most weekdays, yes. We keep some times free each morning. Book online from 7:30 am or call when we open.

Do you do telehealth?

Yes, by phone or video, for follow-ups, results and scripts. Your doctor will tell you if you need to come in.

How do I get my results?

Your doctor will contact you if anything needs action. You can also book a short telehealth call to go through them.

I don't have Medicare. Can I still see a doctor?

Yes. You pay the private fee on the day, and we give you a receipt for your insurer.

Can I get a medical certificate?

Yes, after a consultation in person or by telehealth.

Do I need to fast before a blood test?

Only for some tests. Your doctor will tell you, and our on-site pathology team can check when you book.

How do I move my records from another clinic?

Sign a transfer form at reception and we request them for you.

Can I bring an interpreter?

Yes. We can also book a free phone interpreter for you. Just ask when you book.

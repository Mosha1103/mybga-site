What to expect on your first Sunday

Finding us

12 Example Street, Kellyville NSW. Free parking on site, with spaces near the door for accessibility. Doors open 30 minutes early.

What it is like

About 75 minutes of singing, a short talk and prayer. Wear what is comfortable. Tea and coffee afterwards, and someone will say hello.

For your kids

Supervised kids' program during the 10:45 service for ages 0 to 12. Our team hold current Working With Children Checks.
